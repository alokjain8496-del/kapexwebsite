Garage Permit Calgary: Rules, Setbacks & Requirements

Building or renovating a garage in Calgary can involve building safety approval and, depending on the design and property, additional requirements. This guide gives homeowners a practical starting point before planning construction.

Do You Need a Permit for a Garage in Calgary?

For a detached garage or similar accessory residential structure, Calgary's requirements depend on the project. A residential improvement project permit is generally required for constructing or renovating a detached garage. Structures over 10 m² (107 sq. ft.) require building safety approval, while attached garages are treated differently because they are part of the home or an addition.

Detached Garage: Key Property Considerations

Before finalizing a design, review the property's land-use information, setbacks, building location, separation from the house, lot coverage and any registered maintenance access right-of-way. A detached accessory building larger than 10 m² generally requires 1.0 m clearance from the main house unless another approval path applies.

Trade Permits

Additional trade permits may be required. For a new detached garage, electrical work for lighting and connecting an overhead garage door requires the appropriate electrical permit. Gas, plumbing or other work can also trigger additional permit requirements.

What Is Commonly Needed for an Application?

Calgary's garage application guidance commonly calls for project and property details plus supporting plans. A site plan can need to show the property lines, house and proposed garage, dimensions, distances, openings, roof-truss direction, driveway or apron details, and garage height. Additional documents can be required depending on the project.

What Happens After You Apply?

Once a permit is issued, required inspections must be completed before the project is considered finished. For many residential improvement projects, a framing or rough-in inspection is part of the process, although the exact inspection requirements can vary.
