How Much Does a Basement Suite Cost in Calgary? 2026 Guide

August 26, 2026 Kapex Infra Team Calgary, Alberta
Basement SuitesCost GuideCalgary Permits

A legal basement suite can add flexible living space and rental potential, but the total cost depends heavily on the existing basement and the work required to meet current safety and permit requirements.

What affects the cost of a basement suite?

  • Existing conditions and corrective work
  • Layout, plumbing and utility relocation
  • Egress windows and access
  • Fire and life-safety upgrades
  • Electrical, heating and ventilation work
  • Kitchen, bathroom and finish selections

Typical project budget ranges

A straightforward conversion with a favourable layout can cost less than a project requiring major plumbing relocation, enlarged windows, structural work or extensive utility upgrades. A project-specific scope should be used before relying on any estimate.

Calgary permits and fees in 2026

Every secondary suite requires a building permit. The City of Calgary currently lists published building-permit fees for new suites and existing-suite legalization; additional trade or development approvals can depend on the property and scope.

New suite vs. legalizing an existing suite

Legalizing an existing suite can reduce some construction work, but older work may reveal compliance gaps involving exits, windows, electrical, plumbing, mechanical systems or fire separation.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a permit for a basement suite in Calgary?

Yes. Every secondary suite requires a building permit, and additional approvals may apply depending on the property and scope.

Can an existing basement suite be legalized?

Many existing suites can be assessed for legalization, with required upgrades based on current conditions and applicable requirements.
